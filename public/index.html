Counseling

Couples and marriage counseling is the heart of the practice. Individual work supports it: anxiety, trauma, divorce recovery, and the body’s side of emotion.

    Who I work with

    Couples, individuals, people in the middle of a divorce, and clinicians. Each brings a different question. Each needs the same thing: a safe place to say what is true.

    I am here as

    Change starts with one honest conversation.

    What to expect

    Therapy is a participatory process. You take part in the plan and in judging the progress. Here is how it usually goes.

    Cultivating a lasting relationship is like cultivating a garden. Plants must be fed, watered, and tended to or they will die. In much the same way, relationships require loving care in order to thrive and evolve.
